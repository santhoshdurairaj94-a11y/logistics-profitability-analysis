# Logistics Profitability & Operational Efficiency Analysis (Power BI)

## Overview
This project analyzes logistics operations with a focus on profitability, cost structure, and route-level efficiency using Power BI.

The dashboard is designed to support executive decision-making by answering:
- Are we profitable overall?
- Where are costs concentrated?
- Which routes are efficient or inefficient?
- What operational areas require optimization?

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Profit & Profit Margin
- Revenue per Mile
- Cost per Mile

## Dashboard Structure
### Page 1 – Executive Overview
- High-level KPIs (Revenue, Cost, Profit, Margin)
- Monthly revenue vs cost trend
- Route-level profitability and efficiency

### Page 2 – Operational Insights
- Fuel cost distribution by route
- Cost per mile comparison across origin cities
- Relationship between distance traveled, fuel consumption, and revenue

## Tools & Skills
- Power BI
- DAX (Measures & Calculations)
- Data Modeling
- Business & Operational Analysis

## Key Takeaway
High revenue does not always imply high efficiency. Certain routes consume disproportionately higher fuel for comparable distances, making them priority candidates for operational optimization.

## Notes
The dataset used is synthetic and created for analytical and portfolio purposes.
